Introduction

A canadian born artist residing in Finland, creating a solo career by writing and performing acoustic prog-rock. Currently working on his debut album "Post Pandemic Wartime Rock", with songs, such as: "Sun Goes Boom" & "Fake Saviours".
Darian Bailey is known for his unique chord selection and the sudden, but fitting, changes into different riffs, as the essence of progressive music suggests. Along with the skillful guitar playing, the artist's voice is impactful and bellowing, delivering the deep message societal issues we can all observe in the world today.
